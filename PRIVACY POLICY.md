# Privacy Policy

The Development reference runtime stores submitted operational signals only in process memory and does not implement analytics or external telemetry export.

Signal attributes must be minimized to the operational purpose. Obvious secret-bearing attribute names are rejected, but callers remain responsible for Privacy Shield compliance and for avoiding raw user content, credentials, and unnecessary personal information.

Future durable telemetry, collectors, retention, sharing, dashboards, or external integrations require explicit privacy review and updated acceptance evidence before production use.
