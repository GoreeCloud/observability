# Planned Features

All entries remain unimplemented or unaccepted unless later moved to `IMPLEMENTED-FEATURES.md` with repository evidence.

- **OBS-IDENTITY-001 — Identity-authenticated ingestion:** workload identity, scoped credentials, rotation/revocation, and trusted actor/service context.
- **OBS-MESH-001 — Mesh transport:** registered telemetry producers, dependency topology, delivery receipts, and bounded correlation.
- **OBS-MANAGER-001 — Manager integration:** administrative health feeds and diagnostics without transferring evidence authority.
- **OBS-POLICY-001 — Policy correlation:** policy-service health and decision correlation without redefining policy semantics.
- **OBS-PRIVACY-001 — Privacy Shield controls:** telemetry minimization, retention, purpose, and privacy evidence acceptance.
- **OBS-WARDVEIL-001 — Wardveil controls:** security-safe ingestion, tamper resistance, and security interpretation handoff.
- **OBS-EVERKEEP-001 — Continuity:** durable operational-evidence backup/restore and retention recovery where required.
- **OBS-STORE-001 — Durable signal storage:** versioned metrics/logs/events/traces model with bounded retention and portability.
- **OBS-ALERT-001 — Alerts/SLOs:** governed alert conditions, service-level indicators, history, and incident context.
- **OBS-STABLE-001 — Stable qualification:** exact-release load/failure/privacy/security/recovery/deployment acceptance and production evidence.
